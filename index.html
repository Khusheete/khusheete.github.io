Who am I ?

Hallo 👋🏽 ! I'm Ferdinand, also known as Khusheete. My pronouns are he/him or they/them, whichever you prefer :D. I am a French computer graphics student, and indie game developper. I make small games, and random programming projects to understaind how stuff works under the hood, or just to implement some cool thing or idea I found.

My games can be found on my itch.io page. Most of them are game jam games that may or may not have been worked on afterwards.

I learned programming on my own by creating small games, and visualisation inspired by youtube channels such as The Coding Train, Numberphile, and 3blue1brown... I started with Microsoft Small Basic and then used Processing for a while. After that, I learned many other languages (Java, C++, Rust, odin...) to see how they work. As of today, I mostly use the fantastic Godot Game Engine for game development. And I oscillate between C++ and rust for my other projects.
